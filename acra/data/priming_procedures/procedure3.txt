A dry tube equipped with a stirring bar was charged with the 3H-azepine-2-amine
(1.0 equiv.), PtO2 (10 mol%) and Pd/C (10 mol%). Anhydrous EtOH (0.1 M) was
added, and the tube was transferred to a 150 ml stainless-steel autoclave with
stirring (750 r.p.m.). The autoclave was pressurized/depressurized (×3) with N2
(30 atm) and then with H2 (30 atm; ×3). The autoclave was pressurized with H2
(50 atm), and the mixture was stirred at r.t. for 16 h. The autoclave was
depressurized, and the mixture was filtered through a nylon syringe filter
(0.2 μM), washing with MeOH. The solvent was evaporated, and the crude was
purified by column chromatography on silica gel.
