A solution of the 3H-azepine-2-amine (1.0 equiv.) in anhydrous THF (0.3 M) under
N2 was cooled to 0 °C and treated with BF3·Et2O (1.5 equiv.). The reaction was
allowed to warm to r.t. and stirred for 1 h. The mixture was cooled to −78 °C,
and the organolithium reagent (1.5 equiv.) was added dropwise. The reaction was
stirred at –78 °C for 30 min and then warmed to r.t. and stirred for 30 min. The
mixture was diluted with Et2O and a saturated aqueous solution of K2CO3. The
layers were separated, and the aqueous layer was extracted with Et2O (×3). The
combined organic layers were washed with brine, dried (MgSO4), filtered and
evaporated. The crude was purified by column chromatography on silica gel.
